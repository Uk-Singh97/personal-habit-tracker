How we count

Scheduled day
A day on or after the habit's start date (and before any end date) that matches its chosen weekdays.
Completion percentage
Completed scheduled days divided by scheduled days, rounded. When nothing was scheduled, a dash is shown instead of a number.
Streak
Consecutive scheduled days completed, counted back from today. Days when the habit is not scheduled are skipped and never break a streak. If today is scheduled but not done yet, the streak is not broken until the day ends.
Missed
A scheduled day before today with no completion. Today's unfinished habits count as remaining, not missed.
Limits
The app does not remember old schedules. If you change a habit's schedule, the past is recalculated using the new one, and completions on days that are no longer scheduled are kept but not counted.
