What to expect

While the app is open
The app checks every 30 seconds. A habit is reminded once per day when its reminder time has passed, it is scheduled today, and it is not done yet. You always get a message in the app, and a pop-up as well if you have allowed notifications.
When the app is closed
Nothing. Reminders for a closed tab or browser would need a background service and a push server, which this project does not have. If you open the app after a reminder time with the habit still not done, you get that reminder once when the page loads.
Late reminders
Browsers slow down background tabs, so a reminder can arrive a little late. Computer settings such as Focus Assist or Do Not Disturb can hide pop-ups.
Phones and permissions
Some phone browsers do not allow notifications from web pages. If you press Block, the browser will not ask again. You can change it in your browser's site settings.

Your data

Stored in this browser only, for the open profile. A backup file holds your habits and completion records. The recent activity list and reminder history are not included.
